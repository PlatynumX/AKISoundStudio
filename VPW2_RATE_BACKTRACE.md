# VPW2 rate backtrace

The full generated reports produced during validation are distributed separately from the
source package:

- `vpw2_rom_backtraced_sample_rates.csv`
- `vpw2_backtraced_missing_rates.csv`
- `vpw2_rom_backtraced_sample_rates.txt`

The application can reproduce the full metadata CSV after opening the retail VPW2 ROM.
No ROM data or decoded game audio is included in this repository.
